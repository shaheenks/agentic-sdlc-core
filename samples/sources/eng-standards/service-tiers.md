# Service tiers

- **Tier 1**: customer-facing or money-moving services (payments-api, ledger-service,
  processor-gateway, job-runner). Need an SLO, a runbook, 24x7 on-call and a canary deploy.
- **Tier 2**: internal tools and batch jobs. Business-hours on-call.
- Changing a service's tier needs approval from the platform team.

# Lens: style (technical writing)

Look only at the writing, not at whether the proposal is right.

- **Plain words:** jargon, buzzwords or long words where a short one works ("utilise" → "use",
  "leverage" → "use", "in order to" → "to").
- **Short sentences:** sentences that carry several ideas or need a second read; split them.
- **Defined terms:** an acronym or name used before it is defined, or defined differently in two
  places.
- **Consistent names:** the same thing called by several names (service, component, system), or
  one name used for two things.
- **No filler or hedging:** "basically", "it should be noted that", "we might possibly consider";
  sentences that say nothing; vague quantities ("some", "a few") where a number is known.
- **Active and direct:** say who does what ("the gateway verifies the signature", not "the
  signature is verified"), and give instructions as steps.

Severity: blocker = wording makes a requirement or decision ambiguous; major = inconsistent names
or undefined terms a reader will trip on; nit = wording or sentence length.
